I am a Master's student specializing in fundamental mathematics (*Mathématiques et applications*) at Sorbonne Université in Paris. Driven by a deep passion for alegbra, topology and geometry, my current academic interests lie at the intersection of algebraic topology, algebraic geometry and category theory. 

I recently completed a semester where I ranked 5th out of 137 students overall in the mathematics cohort at Sorbonne Université.

## Research & Projects
My foundational research experience includes a Master 1 research project (TER) focused on the Thurston Norm on the homology of 3-manifolds, conducted under the supervision of Bram Petri. 

My academic goal is to acquire a rigorous theoretical foundation during my Master 2 in Fundamental Mathematics to pursue a PhD in mathematics, focusing on the bridge between homotopy theory and scheme theory.
